## Why hold a launch meetup?

Introduce the community, listen to member expectations and propose short demonstrations of Mistral AI use cases.

## What is planned

- **Free access** to the community and all meetups.
- **Monthly gatherings**, alternating online and in person.
- **In-person events in Montréal and Québec**, alternating between AFI Expertise locations subject to logistical confirmation.
- **Bilingual FR-CA and EN-CA welcome**.

**The date, time, exact venue and registration form are not available yet.** This is an early announcement, not a ticket or reservation.
