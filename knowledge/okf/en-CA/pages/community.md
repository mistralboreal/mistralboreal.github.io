## Why Mistral Boreal?

Because adopting AI takes more than product announcements. It takes skills, verifiable use cases, thoughtful governance and peer-to-peer conversation.

## Our principles

- **Open and free**: no membership or attendance fees.
- **Genuinely bilingual**: information, invitations and resources in Canadian French and Canadian English.
- **Technology independence**: Mistral AI is our focus, but we compare tools on merit and limitations, without dismissing alternatives.
- **Practical sharing**: methods, examples, demonstrations and transferable lessons.
- **Respect and responsibility**: data security, transparency, inclusion and sound practices.

## Who is welcome?

Anyone exploring AI at work or in learning: business leaders, technical experts, educators, developers, researchers and beginners.

## How can you contribute?

Propose a demo, a field report, an article, a learning activity or help with translation on the [Contribute](/en-ca/contribute/) page.
