## Why Mistral Boreal?

Because adopting AI takes more than product announcements. It takes skills, verifiable use cases, thoughtful governance and peer-to-peer conversation.

## Our principles

- **Open and free**: no membership or attendance fees.
- **Genuinely bilingual**: information, invitations and resources in Canadian French and Canadian English.
- **Technology independence**: Mistral AI is our focus, but we compare tools on merit and limitations, without dismissing alternatives.
- **Practical sharing**: methods, examples, demonstrations and transferable lessons.
- **Respect and responsibility**: data security, transparency, inclusion and sound practices.

## Who is welcome?

Anyone exploring AI at work or in learning: business leaders, technical experts, educators, developers, researchers and beginners.

## How can you contribute?

Propose a demo, a field report, an article, a learning activity or help with translation on the [Contribute](/en-ca/contribute/) page.

## Our status with Mistral AI

**We are independent and, as of today, not an official Mistral AI community.** We do not claim accreditation, partnership or sponsorship. Our ambition is to create enough value and trust to **seek official recognition in the future**, subject to Mistral AI's own decision.

Independence also lets us compare technologies freely. Our [Mistral AI product information](/en-ca/ecosystem/) is sourced from the company's own documentation.

## How this site is maintained

GitHub hosts the code and content, with GitHub Actions publishing changes. Initial versions were created with ChatGPT assistance. **We are preparing to move writing workflows to Vibe Work (formerly Le Chat) and code maintenance to Vibe Code.** That transition is still in progress: we do not claim that these tools already maintain the whole website.

Our [open-source blueprint and starter kit](/en-ca/blueprint/) explain how other user groups can apply the same methods.
