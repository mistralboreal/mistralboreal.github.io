## How to use the library

Choose a learning path and follow the goals and exercises prepared by the community. Original Mistral AI materials are linked directly rather than copied into our repository.

We prioritize official documentation, identify its source, and encourage members to check the applicable licence and terms before reusing screenshots, excerpts or materials.

## Planned topics

- Explore Mistral AI products and interfaces.
- Make your first API calls safely.
- Evaluate quality, limitations and cost.
- Compare architectures, data protection and integration options.
- Develop reproducible demonstrations step by step.

The paths listed below are starting points and will evolve as members contribute.
