## A community built around real-world use, not marketing

Mistral Boreal brings together people who want to understand Mistral AI products and put them to work: professionals, developers, leaders, trainers, students and curious newcomers.

We focus on **repeatable demos**, **first-hand experience**, practical experiments and fair comparisons with other technology options. No single platform is pushed, and no prior expertise is required.

## One free gathering every month

Meetups alternate **one month online, the next month in person**. In-person meetings are planned at **AFI Expertise** locations, alternating **between Montréal and Québec**, subject to confirmed arrangements. The cycle shown above illustrates the format, not booked calendar dates.

## Two languages, one community

Every page, article, invitation and resource is offered in **Canadian French (FR-CA)** and **Canadian English (EN-CA)**. Both language communities are welcome at our meetups.

## Independent community

Mistral Boreal **is not an official Mistral AI community**. We currently have no official affiliation, recognition or sponsorship. We **hope to become an officially recognized community one day**, if Mistral AI is interested. We cite and link to [official Mistral AI sources](/en-ca/ecosystem/) rather than claiming to own them.

## Our open-source blueprint

This site uses **GitHub Pages, Astro + Velocity and OKF**, with automated bilingual publishing. **Our content creation and maintenance model is built around the Mistral AI ecosystem**: Vibe Work (formerly Le Chat) and Vibe Code are our reference tools, with human supervision and GitHub Actions checks. We share this approach in our [reusable community blueprint](/en-ca/blueprint/).
