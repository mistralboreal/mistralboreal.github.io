## Our monthly rhythm

| Meetup | Format | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Online | Virtual |
| 2 | In person | AFI Expertise — Montréal |
| 3 | Online | Virtual |
| 4 | In person | AFI Expertise — Québec |

This four-meeting pattern repeats. **It describes the proposed rotation, not confirmed dates**. Each invitation will separately provide the date, time, room, accessibility details and free registration link.

## What to expect

Guided explorations of Mistral AI products, model and API experiments, workplace use cases, comparisons with other tools, governance discussions and open Q&A.

## Language accessibility

Announcements and written materials are provided in both Canadian languages. Session-specific language arrangements will appear in each invitation.

## Community-led sessions

Suggest a topic, co-host a demonstration or share an experiment that did not go as expected. [Propose a topic](/en-ca/contribute/).
