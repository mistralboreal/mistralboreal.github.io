## A practical, independent editorial approach

We publish guides, demonstrations, field reports and technology decision analyses. Comparisons should explain context, evaluation criteria and limitations.

Every article has linked Canadian French and Canadian English versions. Technical examples must not contain API keys, confidential information or unsupported claims.
