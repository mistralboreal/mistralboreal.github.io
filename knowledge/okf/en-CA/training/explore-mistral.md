## Learning goals

After this introduction, you should be able to recognize major product categories and decide whether to start with a conversational tool, a developer platform or an API.

## Suggested activity

1. Open the [official Mistral AI documentation](https://docs.mistral.ai/).
2. Find the sections intended for end users and developers.
3. Describe a personal or professional use case to explore.
4. List questions about data protection, quality and cost.
5. Share one learning at a community meetup.

**Source**: documentation maintained by Mistral AI. Product descriptions may change; rely on the official source.
